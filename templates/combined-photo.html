How to Combine Two Photos Easily with CutCompress

What is the Combine Photos Tool?

Do you have two photos you want to put together? Maybe you want to make a special picture with a friend or family member. The Combine Photos tool on CutCompress helps you do this quickly. It takes two pictures and joins them side by side into one image. You don’t need any hard software or skills—just a few clicks, and you’re done!

This tool is great for making passport-size photos or just keeping memories in one frame. It’s free, easy, and works online, so anyone can use it. Let’s learn how to use it and why it’s helpful.

How to Use the Tool: Simple Steps

Here’s how you can combine your photos:

  1. Upload First Photo: On the page, you’ll see two boxes. Click the first box or drag your photo into it. The tool will remove the background automatically.
  2. Upload Second Photo: Do the same for the second box. Drop or click to add your next photo. Wait a moment while it cleans up the background.
  3. Combine Them: When both photos are ready, click the “Combine Photos” button. The tool will put them together in one picture.
  4. Download: After combining, you’ll see the final image. Click “Download” to save it to your device.

It’s that simple! The tool does most of the work for you.

Tips to Get the Best Combined Photo

To make your photo look perfect, try these ideas:

  • Use Portrait Photos: Photos that are taller than they are wide work best. They fit nicely into the passport-size frame.
  • Good Lighting: Make sure both photos have clear light. Dark or blurry pictures might not look as good.
  • Simple Backgrounds: The tool removes backgrounds, but plain ones (like a wall) are easier to clean up.
  • Same Size: If possible, use photos that are about the same size. This makes the final image look balanced.

Follow these tips, and your combined photo will look professional!
