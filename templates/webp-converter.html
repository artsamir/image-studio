What is WebP?

WebP is a type of picture format created by Google. It’s designed to make pictures smaller without losing their quality. Smaller pictures are great because they load quickly on websites and don’t take up much space on your computer, phone, or tablet. Think of it like packing a suitcase: WebP fits more into less space, but everything still looks just as good.

Regular picture formats like JPG or PNG can be large and slow. WebP is different—it shrinks them down and even adds features like see-through backgrounds or movement, which some other formats can’t do. It’s a smart way to handle pictures in today’s fast-moving online world.

How WebP Stacks Up Against Other Formats

Not all picture formats are the same. Here’s a table to show how WebP compares:

Format Size Clear Background? Movement?
JPG Medium to Large No No
PNG Large Yes No
GIF Medium Yes Yes
WebP Small Yes Yes

JPG works well for photos but doesn’t support clear backgrounds or movement. PNG is good for sharp designs with transparency, but it takes up a lot of space. GIF can move, but it’s not the smallest. WebP stands out because it’s small and can do more than the others.

What Happens During Conversion?

When you use the WebP Converter, it changes your picture in a few ways:

  • Size Drops: A 1MB JPG might become a 700KB WebP—less space, same look.
  • Quality Stays: Even though it’s smaller, the picture doesn’t get blurry or messy.
  • New Features: A JPG can gain a clear background, or a PNG can shrink without losing sharpness.

It’s like squeezing a sponge—it gets smaller but still holds its shape. This makes your pictures better for websites or sharing.
